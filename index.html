Research Overview

I study how people make decisions under uncertainty, using experimental and field methods to understand consumer choice, incentive design, and policy communication.

My work bridges behavioral science and applied economics, with a focus on translating evidence into practical recommendations for institutions and public services.

Current projects explore decision-making in digital marketplaces, climate policy engagement, and the role of trust in economic interactions.
